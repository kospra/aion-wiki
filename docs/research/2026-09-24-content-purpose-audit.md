# Content purpose and article structure audit

Date: 2026-09-24
Status: Research and proposed direction; not an approved implementation specification.

## Reader and intended outcome

The user selected: **Both: learn first, then reference.** Articles should help a new reader understand a mechanic and let a returning reader reach a rule, value, or procedure quickly. Preserve all imported information, image-number/color relationships, source references, regional qualifiers, and uncertainty. Keep Chakra UI and the established minimal visual direction.

## Diagnosis

The current model describes presentation blocks (paragraph, list, figure, table, note, group), but not their purpose. Source order and source-capture evidence dominate the reading sequence. Explanations, author recommendations, procedures, catalogs, worked examples, and provenance receive similar treatment even when they answer different questions.

The audit covers 43 articles plus the source overview (44 entries). There are 321 figure annotation mappings and 658 annotation-to-passage reference associations. These associations are not the original author's bibliography: the imported source has 21 link occurrences. No duplicate exact primary destination tuples were found within an individual mapping; the usability issue is reference fanout and repetition in the reading flow.

Gear anatomy contains 90 rendered blocks, four figures, 17 annotation mappings, and 93 reference associations. Its first stat-layer definition is delayed until block-0034, after a generic uncertainty note, a beginner Source-action tip, and several figures. Theostones has 248 rendered blocks, including nested table structure, but only one heading: proc rules, a large combat sample, the author's assessment, and a cosmetic effect are poorly differentiated.

The previous typography and link-label changes improved individual elements while leaving this source-shaped reading order intact.

## Approaches

1. **Recommended: purpose-based composition inside existing articles.** A short orientation leads into manually curated groups for explanation, action, comparison, lookup, or examples. Preserve existing routes and source anchors. This addresses reading order without creating duplicate encyclopedias.
2. **Keep source order and reduce evidence prominence.** Lower effort, but delayed definitions and unrelated material remain adjacent. Useful as a temporary improvement only.
3. **Separate learning paths and a reference encyclopedia.** Potentially helpful later, but creates more navigation and maintenance obligations. Not needed to resolve the current article problem.

Organizing around reader needs follows [GOV.UK's user-needs guidance](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/plan-manage-content/identify-user-needs/). Distinguishing explanation, procedural guidance, and reference also draws on [Diataxis](https://diataxis.fr/). The five formats below are this project's proposed application of those principles, not a claim that either source prescribes this exact design.

## Purpose determines structure and visual treatment

| Reader job             | Proposed article structure                                              | Chakra presentation direction                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Understand a mechanic  | Orientation, parts and relationships, rules, exceptions                 | Calm prose, meaningful headings, aligned diagrams and definitions                                    |
| Choose between options | Decision context, comparable options, conditions, source recommendation | Comparisons with adequate explanation width; recommendation distinguished from observed usage        |
| Follow a procedure     | Goal, prerequisites, risks, steps, result, example                      | Numbered steps; materials next to their step; destructive consequences before action                 |
| Look up values         | Scope, categories, consistent fields, exceptions                        | Compact structured rows or tables only where fields truly compare; long explanations remain readable |
| Interpret an example   | Inputs and assumptions, observation/calculation, conclusion, limits     | Clearly labeled example; large breakdown secondary; assumptions visible beside numbers               |

These are flexible formats, not five mandatory boxes on every page. Mixed articles can contain several purposeful sections. Class Passives remains an honest incomplete-content placeholder.

## Information hierarchy

- **Main explanation:** rules, definitions, decisions, necessary values, and procedure steps. Readable without opening disclosures.
- **Supporting example:** screenshots and samples that clarify the explanation. Introduce what each picture proves or illustrates before its detailed annotations.
- **Evidence and source details:** attribution, capture notes, annotation-to-passage links, and repeated provenance. Available in clearly labeled secondary disclosures rather than a wall of links.
- **Critical qualifications:** regional scope, uncertain numbers, contradictory evidence, losses, and date-sensitive limits stay beside the claim they affect. They are never hidden as generic source metadata.

Use Chakra Heading, Text, Stack, semantic lists, Table where comparable, and Accordion for optional evidence. Whitespace and headings establish primary grouping. Borders and backgrounds distinguish real boundaries; avoid enclosing every paragraph in another card. Color supports meaning but never replaces text labels or numeric diagram keys. Keep the neutral theme and established reading width.

A short orientation states what the article helps the reader do. Meaningful section links let returning readers jump directly to values or steps. Navigation should reflect those sections rather than the source document's incidental heading structure.

## Concrete article redesigns

### Gear anatomy

Reader question: What are the seven tooltip layers, and what survives transfer?

Proposed order: short orientation; numbered gear diagram aligned with the seven layer explanations; transfer-survival comparison; separate Finding an item's source tip; evidence details. Keep Armor's DOUBLE CHECK and the unresolved Asia/Global Potential distinction next to affected layers. Screenshot colors and numbers must remain paired with their actual meanings, not decorative categories.

### Gear transfer

Proposed order: goal and eligibility; donor/recipient roles and donor-consumption warning; what carries over; same-grade versus cross-grade material requirements; fragment-to-stone recipes; screenshot interpretation; remaining source limits. Separate inventory held from materials required. A displayed successes count is not a material cost.

### Theostones

Proposed order: socket and proc rules; non-stacking behavior; clearly attributed author assessment; a combat example with its own assumptions and limitations; cosmetic glow. Keep the detailed combat breakdown available. A rounded 0% row still reports 626,330 damage; the sample lacks enough encounter/build/duration context to establish universal DPS value.

### Wings

The catalog uses name, Equip effects, Owned effects, and scaling information consistently. Class usage is a separate dated observation, not a universal ranking. Preserve KR scope and the 8/21/2026 observation date; distinguish predicted future usage from recorded percentages.

### Genus and formulas

Separate the Insight/lock procedure from stat-priority lookup. Keep Global Line 4 advice distinct from older Asia Lines 3/9 Auto Analysis. Formula pages put assumptions beside inputs and results. Preserve ambiguous literal expressions and unknowns rather than presenting them as verified calculators.

## Preservation and implementation constraints for the eventual spec

Curate semantic groups explicitly against canonical source block IDs; do not infer them solely from headings, strings, or block length. Preserve the imported source representation and existing deep links. Every source block, figure placement, annotation mapping, highlight, uncertainty, and reference needs an accounted-for destination. Opening a deep link into secondary evidence should reveal its containing disclosure.

Existing audits enforce source fidelity and rendering behavior. Changing composition or disclosure visibility requires deliberate audit updates with equivalent coverage, not weakening checks to accommodate missing content. Distinguish structural preservation from universal initial visibility. Do not silently resolve contradictions or invent gameplay steps.

Acceptance should include full source coverage, correct image/text associations, regional and numeric fidelity, usable section navigation, keyboard-accessible evidence disclosures, narrow-screen reading, and representative checks of every article format. This research phase changes no product code and makes no new gameplay accuracy claims.

## Cross-article reading issues that matter for a purpose-led design

1. **Source ordering delays the useful question.** Gear anatomy presents the Source-action tip and figures 003–006 before the first layer definition at `block-0034`; figure 005 has labels 1–6 and figure 006 holds 7, so the reader must shuttle between image and seven list sections. A purpose-led entry should answer “what am I looking at?” first, then let each layer expand to detail. Similarly, the raid targets in `block-0347`–`block-0359` come after conditional Accuracy choices in `block-0309`/`block-0316`, even though the raid goal supplies the reason to choose Accuracy.
2. **One source chapter is not one reader job.** Chapter 02 mixes comparison cards (Weapon/Armor/Accessories) with special-slot procedures (Belt/Amulet, Runes) and Pendant lookup. Chapter 05 mixes steps, a choice guide, a combat example, and a transfer recipe. The article archetype is the useful styling unit; chapter membership can remain navigation/provenance.
3. **Cross-links often repeat context already on the destination path.** `block-0062`–`block-0065` preview Sync/Reset/Bind, `block-0232`–`block-0246` give the reroll strategy, and `block-0480` sends readers back to chapter 03. The priorities article duplicates the rarity/scope context as `context-block-0248` and `context-block-0250` plus `reference-block-0248`/`reference-block-0250`. A source-reference link and a one-sentence cue are enough in a main reading path; full repeated framing can live in provenance.
4. **Annotation fanout can make one illustration feel like many competing explanations.** Figure 005 has seven mapping labels and 38 `textSourceIds`; figure 006 has five mappings and 37 references. Figures 042 and 043 are separate placements of the same Soul Binding artwork and each has eight stat mappings. These are valid evidence links, but repeating labels, source links, and explanatory prose around each row increases scan burden. Keep a concise legend beside the figure and expose detailed mapping/provenance only on demand. This is fanout and cognitive repetition, not a claim that identical destination URLs are duplicated.
5. **Worked screens need a different visual contract from rules.** `growth-stage-context` prevents treating figure 026's sample 25.5% as the +15→+16 rule; `theostone-combat-context` prevents reading figure 039's rounded 0% as no damage; `transfer-direction`/`transfer-material-context` prevent reading recipient pre-transfer values or “successes: 5” as resulting stats or material counts. Put rule, example reading, and its limitations in visually distinct groups.
6. **Unknowns should remain adjacent to the claim they qualify.** Examples: Armor Amp “DOUBLE CHECK” (`block-0051`), Rune +9 Multi-hit (`block-0209`), Muspel Normal question marks (`block-0355`–`block-0357`), crafted Potential on Global (`block-0424`), Arcana EXP loss amount (`context-block-0563`), and eight-card Parchment allocation (`context-block-0629`). An omnibus top warning is weaker than a local caveat at the affected figure, number, or recommendation.

## Purpose-led styling implications

- **Understand mechanic:** annotated diagram/brief definition → the few rules → edge cases. A seven-part tooltip should have seven directly addressable parts; a three-control system should compare Bind, Sync, Reset before explaining each.
- **Choose/compare:** put options on common dimensions (slot, benefit, skill line, material/risk), then source recommendations and local caveats. Never sort roll rarity as though it were damage value.
- **Follow procedure:** prerequisites/materials → action → result/irreversible loss → example UI. Keep action cost apart from inventory counts.
- **Lookup catalogue:** compact rows with consistent fields and quick filtering/scanning; keep regional scope and approximate status visible near values.
- **Interpret worked example:** show what figure proves, what prose adds, and what cannot be generalized. Theostone damage, Amp chance, and transfer screenshots need this treatment even when embedded in articles with another primary archetype.
